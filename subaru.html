Subaru

Impreza WRX STI

The Subaru Impreza WRX STI is more than a high-performance car—it's a roaring manifesto of rally spirit, engineering grit, and pure passion. Born from Subaru's fierce success in the World Rally Championship during the 1990s and early 2000s, it became the embodiment of every gearhead's dream. Behind its aggressive hood scoop, bulging fenders, and gold wheels, the STI carries an aura that's equal parts rebellion and precision.

This is a car molded by competition—from its symmetrical AWD system to its turbocharged boxer engine and track-based dynamics. When you drive an STI, you're not just gripping the wheel—you're holding onto a legacy forged in dirt, snow, and podium finishes. Even decades after its heyday, the WRX STI remains a reflection of what happens when motorsport DNA meets everyday driveability.

Specs (Subaru Impreza WRX STI 2003-2007)

  • Engine: 2.0 L turbocharged Flat-4 (EJ207, JDM) / 2.5 L turbocharged Flat-4 (EJ257, export)
  • Power: 265-300 hp
  • Torque: 343-392 Nm (253-289 lb-ft)
  • Transmission: 6-speed manual
  • Drivetrain: Symmetrical AWD with Driver-Controlled Center Differential (DCCD in later models)
  • 0-60 mph: ~5.2-5.5 seconds
  • Top Speed: ~151-158 mph (244-254 km/h)
