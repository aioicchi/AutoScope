Nissan

GT-R R34

The Nissan Skyline GT-R R34 isn't just a machine — it's a myth forged in steel and turbocharged air. Born in 1999, it carried the weight of the Skyline bloodline and cemented itself as the king of JDM legends. To enthusiasts, the R34 is not just a car to drive, but a symbol of aspiration — the poster on bedroom walls, the hero of video games, the car that defined a generation's love for speed. Its presence alone carries an aura of reverence.

Beneath its iconic Bayside Blue skin lives the RB26DETT, a 2.6L twin-turbo inline-six with an understated rating of 280 hp — though in reality, it was capable of far more. With the ATTESA E-TS Pro all-wheel drive and Super HICAS rear-wheel steering, the R34 wasn't just fast, it was calculated precision. It became the car that tuners worshipped, drivers respected, and rivals feared.

Decades later, the R34 remains untouchable, a timeless icon whose name echoes louder with every passing year. It is not simply a Skyline — it is the Godzilla that roared across the world.

Specs (Nissan Skyline GT-R R34)

  • Engine: 2.6L RB26DETT Twin-Turbo Inline-6
  • Power: ~280 hp (official rating, far higher when tuned)
  • Torque: 392 Nm (289 lb-ft)
  • Transmission: 6-speed Getrag manual
  • Drivetrain: ATTESA E-TS Pro AWD + Super HICAS
  • 0-60 mph: ~4.9s | Top Speed: ~165 mph (266 km/h)
