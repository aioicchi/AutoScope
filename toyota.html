Supra MK4

The Mk4 Supra isn't just another JDM legend—it's the benchmark for Japanese performance. Built between 1993 and 2002, it's best known for housing the iconic 2JZ-GTE twin-turbo inline-six, an engine so overbuilt it's capable of handling 1,000+ hp with the right mods.

From factory, the Supra pushed around 320 hp in US spec, but anyone in the scene knows that was just the beginning. The Getrag V160 6-speed manual, rear-wheel drive layout, and timeless coupe design made it the perfect platform for tuning, whether it's drag, drift, or high-speed builds.

The Mk4 became a legend thanks to its balance of bulletproof reliability, insane tuning potential, and motorsport pedigree. Throw in its unforgettable role in Fast & Furious, and you've got a car that isn't just respected—it's worshipped in car culture.

Technical specs:

  • Engine: 3.0L twin-turbo inline-6 (2JZ-GTE)
  • Stock Power: 326 hp / 325 lb-ft (JDM)
  • Transmission: Getrag V160 6-speed manual or 4-speed auto
  • Drivetrain: RWD
  • Tuning Potential: 800-1200 hp on stock internals (with supporting mods)
  • 0-60 mph: ~4.6 seconds (stock)
  • Top Speed: 155 mph (limited, but capable of much more)
